---
title: "AI development needs its own SOPs, separate from the software development lifecycle"
created: 2026-09-16
tags:
  - "evergreen"
---
Three speakers across both conference days converged on this. AI development and software development are different animals: a generative system has no single expected result, its training data is part of the product, and its behavior varies with model version, infrastructure, data environment, and the human prompting it. Forcing AI into a software development lifecycle SOP produces documents that describe a process nobody follows.

The working shape: dataset development and model development SOPs when training in-house; when using a purchased or public model, pin the version, bound the inputs, and write the procedure around that. On the validation side, keep AI model validation SOPs separate from traditional computerized system validation, and write dedicated procedures for AI testing and validation. One pharmacovigilance team credited its dedicated AI SOPs with reducing deviations.
