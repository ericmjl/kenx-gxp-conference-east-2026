# Companies own the accuracy of AI-drafted submissions regardless of the drafting tool

The MHRA inspectorate (Peter Brown, June 29, 2026 blog post) reported receiving AI-generated inspection responses that cited non-existent guidance. The agency's position: the company is responsible for the accuracy of everything it submits, no matter which tool drafted it, and responses should get technical review from experienced personnel. Disclosure of AI use is encouraged and heading toward expected.

This is the regulatory version of a pattern the whole conference kept hitting. The first AI-related CGMP warning letter went to a company whose defense was that the AI did not tell them a step was needed. The regulator hears that as the company's failure, because it is. Handing drafting to a model transfers none of the accountability.
