---
title: "Human verification of AI output must be explicit because document creation no longer carries implicit verification"
created: 2026-09-16
tags:
  - "evergreen"
---
When a person writes a document, they verify facts, figures, and claims continuously while creating it. When AI drafts the document, that implicit verification vanishes, and most teams never budget for its explicit replacement. This is why human review of AI output takes longer than expected.

The remedy has a regulatory root: 21 CFR 211.68(c) has required a human check that automated equipment performs properly since 2008. The operational version: AI drafts, a named human verifies explicitly and separately, and some of the time AI saves gets budgeted toward that verification. One speaker's motto: "Never Trust! Always Verify!"
