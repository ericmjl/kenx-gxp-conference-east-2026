## Five properties of defensible AI control

A documented intended use that states what the AI cannot do; risk-proportionate validation run as a continuous lifecycle because models drift; ALCOA+ audit trails extended to capture user, model, and version, inputs, outputs, justification, and confidence score; enforced approval by a named accountable human; and continuous lifecycle monitoring.

## Why the phrasing lands

"AI acts, humans decide, and the system remembers everything" separates capability from authority. He reframes human-in-the-loop as human-centric design because nobody genuinely reviews a hundred AI-generated documents; the control has to make the review real, and the memory has to make it provable. AI is not a faster human. His three-step start: inventory every AI touching GxP including shadow AI, enforce the human gate, treat validation as continuous.
