Induced from Bharanitharan Rajendran's human-risk lecture at the KENX AI in GxP East 2026 conference ([[2026-09-15-1024-gorani-ai-governance-human-risk-gxp]]), 2026-09-15.

## Five properties of defensible AI control

A documented intended use that states what the AI cannot do; risk-proportionate validation run as a continuous lifecycle because models drift; ALCOA+ audit trails extended to capture user, model, and version, inputs, outputs, justification, and confidence score; enforced approval by a named accountable human; and continuous lifecycle monitoring. The intended-use property is [[The intended use statement is the master control lever for GxP AI]]; the continuous-lifecycle property is [[Validation is data, not an event]]; the named-human property is [[Human-in-the-loop must be a named hard gate, not rubber-stamp oversight]]. The memory property needs [[Autonomous agents need their own auditable identities in regulated systems]], or the trail has no attributable actor. If an unauthorized change alters a validated result, that is also [[A security failure that changes a validated outcome is a quality failure]].

## Why the phrasing lands

"AI acts, humans decide, and the system remembers everything" separates capability from authority. He reframes human-in-the-loop as human-centric design because nobody genuinely reviews a hundred AI-generated documents; the control has to make the review real, and the memory has to make it provable. [[Human verification of AI output must be explicit because document creation no longer carries implicit verification]] is how the review becomes real; [[An LLM judge is a GxP system and needs its own qualification pack]] can triage but does not decide. AI is not a faster human. His three-step start: inventory every AI touching GxP including shadow AI, enforce the human gate, treat validation as continuous. [[Human error, not AI failure, drives GxP warning letters]] is the same speaker's citation record, and [[Assuming humans will follow SOPs is hope, not control]] is what you have if the gate is only hoped for.
