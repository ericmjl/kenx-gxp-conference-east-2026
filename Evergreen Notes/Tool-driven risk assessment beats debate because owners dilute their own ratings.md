## The incentive problem inside the risk workshop

Room-based risk debates get watered down because nobody wants to own residual risk. The panel states the mechanism from the vendor side: humans on the hook for outcomes are incentivized to dilute ratings. Both talks arrive at the same replacement: objective, tool-driven risk assessment. RiskCortex converts regulatory rules into deterministic, explainable logic through a semantic risk graph, which also makes the assessment reproducible for an inspector.

## What stays human

The tool rates; the accountable human still approves. RiskCortex's Expert in the Loop challenges, approves, and stays accountable with every accept-or-reject decision audit-trailed, and their playbook replaces debate with evidence rather than replacing judgment.
