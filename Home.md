# Home

Notes from the KENX "Harnessing the Power of AI in GxP" conference (AI in GxP East 2026), held in Raleigh, NC on 2026-09-15 and 2026-09-16. Twenty-three sessions captured live on a Plaud recorder: nineteen lectures and panels, one vendor consultation, and three hallway conversations, all transcribed, summarized, and distilled into evergreen notes.

## Maps of Content

- [[Conference Agenda MOC]] - every transcript in chronological order, with speakers and session titles
- [[AI Validation and Acceptance Criteria MOC]] - continuous validation, acceptance criteria, intended use, LLM judging
- [[Data Foundations and Audit Trails MOC]] - governed data estates, agent identity, audit-trail review with local LLMs
- [[Governance Risk and Human Factors MOC]] - warning letters, human risk, regulation, security, and the compliance framework talks
- [[Strategy Scaling and Product MOC]] - pilot-to-production, MVPs in GxP markets, and the vendor landscape
- [[Eric's Field Notes MOC]] - hallway conversations, the audit war-room debrief, and open threads

## Where to start

Three talks anchor the conference's intellectual spine. Bill Gargano's keynote argues [[Validation is data, not an event]]. Abhishek Bachchan's lecture argues [[Validating an AI system in a regulated industry means validating a measurement system]]. Ben O'Brien's lecture argues [[The intended use statement is the master control lever for GxP AI]]. From there, the theme MOCs above fan out into the rest.

## Provenance

- Transcripts are verbatim Plaud captures, lightly corrected for speaker and company names. Diarization and transcription can be wrong; the recordings' words belong to the speakers.
- Each transcript note carries a summary, the app-generated Plaud AI notes, and (where flagged) Eric's own highlights, followed by the timestamped transcript.
- Evergreen notes are Eric's conceptual distillations, titled as claims, each citing the session it was induced from.
