# AI Validation and Acceptance Criteria MOC

The conference's center of gravity: what it means to validate a non-deterministic system. The talks converge on one shift, from validation as a documentation event to validation as a continuous, data-based process, and they disagree only about how to get there.

- [[2026-09-15-1112-ai-observability-evaluation-gxp-lecture|Trust Is Not a Framework (Genari AI)]] - observability versus evaluation; LLM-as-judge run on a different model at low non-zero temperature; judge qualification packs
- [[2026-09-15-1426-abhishek-ai-acceptance-criteria-gxp-lecture|Abhishek Bachchan (Gilead)]] - validating a measurement system; six acceptance-test buckets; evidence as a dashboard with a shelf life

- [[Validation is data, not an event]]
