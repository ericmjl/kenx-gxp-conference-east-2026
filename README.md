# kenx-gxp-conference-east-2026

An Obsidian wiki of the KENX "Harnessing the Power of AI in GxP" conference (AI in GxP East 2026), Raleigh, NC, September 15-16, 2026. Twenty-three sessions captured live on a Plaud recorder, organized as Maps of Content, verbatim transcripts, and evergreen notes.

## Structure

```text
Home.md                          master Map of Content, start here
Maps of Content/                 six MOCs categorizing every transcript
Transcripts/                     23 session notes: summary, AI notes, highlights, verbatim transcript
Evergreen Notes/                 35 claim-titled distillations, each citing its source session
Resources/                       the official agenda compendium PDF
```

## Reading paths

- Chronological: start at [[Maps of Content/Conference Agenda MOC|Conference Agenda MOC]].
- By theme: pick a MOC. Validation and acceptance criteria, data foundations and audit trails, governance and human factors, strategy and product, or Eric's field notes.
- By claim: browse Evergreen Notes; every note is titled as a full-sentence claim and links back to the session it came from.

## Provenance and attribution

- Transcripts are verbatim outputs of a Plaud recorder's speech-to-text, lightly corrected for speaker and company names. Diarization and transcription can be wrong. The spoken words belong to the speakers and their employers; summaries and interpretations of their talks are Eric Ma's.
- Each transcript note contains, in order: an agent-authored summary, the app-generated Plaud AI notes, Eric's own highlights (where he pressed the highlight button), and the timestamped transcript.
- Evergreen notes are Eric Ma's conceptual distillations in his own words, written after the conference from the transcripts.
- No license is granted; this is a personal knowledge base made public for reference.

## Using this repo

Open the folder in [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) as a vault (wikilinks resolve out of the box). Plain markdown works too; every cross-reference is a standard `[[wikilink]]`, most with display aliases.
